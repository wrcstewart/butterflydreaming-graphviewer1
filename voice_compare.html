Trained on this

Prompts 0001–0003 of the Tao set, read into the session itself. A fine-tune reproduces its own sentences best, so this is the upper bound — the most the voice can do, not what it will usually do.

Never heard, same text and register

Prompts 0016–0017 — further into the same translation, in the same voice and rhythm, but not recorded. This is the honest test: it isolates generalisation from memorisation, because nothing but the training set differs.

Never heard, different register

Ordinary modern prose with none of the Tao's cadence. Where a thin fine-tune usually falls down hardest, and the closest of the three to what BD actually reads aloud.

A long sentence — where the comma pause actually appears

BD reads a sentence whole up to 150 characters, and only breaks a longer one at a comma or semicolon. So the three passages above have no comma pauses to hear — every gap in them is a full stop's 504 ms. This one is 254 characters from BD's own corpus, and splits into several fragments with the shorter 180 ms gap between them. Unseen by the voice.
