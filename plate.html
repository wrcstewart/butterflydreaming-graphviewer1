The Plate and the Plan

Reduced to two dimensions: positions are x, y, heading is one angle, and the Kolam is a one-dimensional strip. The question it answers is where the figure sits once a headset owns the camera — and the answer is that it sits exactly where it sits today. What is new is that the viewer acquires a placement.

Every ratio that reaches the eye is identical to the last digit, so the drawing above serves both. A 0.5 m step is 50 plan units in A and 0.5 in B, which looks like a vast difference and is not one. The choice is not about geometry — it is about who gets moved when the script moves something.
