QR for a headset

On a Quest you own:

  1. say “Hey Meta, open QR code scanner” — or open a scanner app you have installed, since the built-in one is still on the Public Test Channel
  2. look at this code through passthrough: on a screen is fine
  3. take the open link action

On a Vision Pro you own: there is no native scanner, so this needs a third-party QR app installed first. On a demo unit in a shop: nothing can be installed and no account is yours, so the URL must be typed — which is why the check page is at /x.
