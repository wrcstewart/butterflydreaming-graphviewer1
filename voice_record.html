How to record — read this first

Setup matters more than quantity. One quiet room, one microphone position, and don't move between clips. Inconsistent conditions poison a fine-tune, because the model learns the room as part of the voice.

  • Hold the button while speaking, release when finished. Leave a breath of silence at each end — don't clip the first or last word.
  • Read at the pace you want back. The model learns delivery, not just timbre: read briskly and everything it ever says will be brisk.
  • Play back the first two or three and listen for room echo, hum, or clipping — the green bar should peak around three-quarters, never full.
  • Fluff it? Just record again before pressing Keep. Only the last take is written, and re-recording a kept clip replaces it.
  • Stop when tired. Progress is saved on the server, so you can resume later — but a tired voice in the middle of a set is worse than a short set.
