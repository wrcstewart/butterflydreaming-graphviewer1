BD speech bench

Can a phone synthesise a corpus passage in the browser, fast enough to be worth having?

Passage

Contains the names that matter: Li Bai, Du Fu, Laozi, Zhuangzi, Tao Te Ching. Listen for how it says them — that is what the lexicon will have to fix.

Run

Each engine downloads a model on first run and caches it. Use wi-fi. Piper voice ~60 MB. Kokoro uses fp32 (~330 MB) where WebGPU exists, q8 (~90 MB) otherwise — that is its own guidance, and forcing q8 onto WebGPU measures the mistake rather than the engine.
