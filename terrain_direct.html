Terrain, direct — height and width straight from the grammar

No angle is computed. One grammar sets the height of each flat top, another its width. The histogram is drawn faintly; the black line joins the midpoints of the tops. The default view is the whole 341 km range at 9.6× vertical; set vertical to 1 for true scale.

Height grammar
How big a > or < is. 0 = dead level plains, higher = rougher ground within a plain.
Which WAY the gentle steps lean. At 0 a <> pair cancels exactly and the plain is level however big the step is — so this, not the slider above, is what tilts a plain.
walk gives slow structure, level gives fast. This is the dial the angle model could not separate.
Width grammar
Smoothing
A moving average over the heights. 1 = untouched. The histogram stays raw, so the line shows what the smoothing did.
