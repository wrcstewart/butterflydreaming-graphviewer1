Lorenz Workbench

The 1963 convection equations, traced

dx/dt = σ(y − x)
dy/dt = x(ρ − z) − y
dz/dt = xy − βz

Starting point

Any change restarts the trace. Arrow keys on a focused slider step by 0.01 — small enough to see the butterfly effect: two starts this close soon follow different paths.

Constants

Lorenz used σ 10, ρ 28, β 8/3. Below ρ ≈ 24.74 the trace settles onto one of the two fixed points; above it, the butterfly. Below ρ 1 everything falls to the origin.

Tracing

A smaller time step is more accurate; steps per frame sets how fast it draws. The oldest points drop off once the trail is full. Below full opacity, places the path passes many times show brighter, so the density of the orbit becomes visible.

Camera

Dragging the view moves these sliders too. Spin turns the camera round the vertical (z) axis, in degrees per second.
